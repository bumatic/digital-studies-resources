## Introduction 

Over the course of the past decades a broad range of approaches to study of digital media was developed such as platform, data, algorithm, software, code, and AI studies. Their shared aim is the analysis of how digital media partake in shaping our contemporary lives, to reflect upon the ongoing becoming of the digital, and to criticize their immanent politics as well as the new forms of power they give way to. 

The notion of *critical digital studies* serves an umbrella term for these approaches. In itself *critical digital studies* does not describe a specific approach to the study of digital media and technologies. It rather constitutes a research attitude towards the digital and its ongoing promise of innovation and transformation, that in particular applies to the volatile and unstable history of digital media themselves.


## Digital Methods

One of the most productive approaches to research in and with digital media is called *digital methods*, proposed by Richard Rogers. As a research program, *digital methods* goes beyond the mere use of digital tools for researching digital media. It rather aims “to learn from the so-called method of the medium, that is, how online devices treat web data” (Rogers 2019, 4). At its core is the dictum to “follow the medium” (Rogers 2009, 10) in order to reveal the specific methods of digital media on the one hand and to appropriate and repurpose them for new approaches in social research on the other hand. 

For following the medium and for studying social and cultural transformations grounded in digital media a broad range of research tools have been developed and made available throughout the past decade. This pages contains references to collections of digital methods tools which are typically maintained by research institutions. Furthermore, it contains a section listing general resources and tutorials regarding digital research. For references to resrouces for specific objects of study, like platforms, software, games and web history see the respective pages.
